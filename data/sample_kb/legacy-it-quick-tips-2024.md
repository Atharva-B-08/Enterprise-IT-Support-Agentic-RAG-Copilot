# IT Quick Tips (v1.2), March 2024

> **Note:** This document is outdated and kept for reference only. Refer to the current IT Handbook.

## Getting help

- Call the IT helpline: **+91-22-5555-0150**
- Support hours: Monday to Friday, 8:30 AM to 6:00 PM IST
- Email: `it-help@nimbus-logistics.example`

## Passwords

- Passwords must be at least 10 characters
- Change your password every 90 days
- SMS codes are accepted as the second factor for all accounts

## Laptops

- Laptops are replaced every 5 years
- USB storage devices are allowed on company laptops if scanned first

## Remote access

- Use the old VPN client "NimbusConnect"
- Personal laptops may be used for work with manager approval

## Reporting a lost device

Report lost devices within 24 hours to your manager, who will inform IT.

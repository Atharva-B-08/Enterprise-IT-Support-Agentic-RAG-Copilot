# RB-003: Printer and Scanner Troubleshooting

## Before you begin

- Printers are available only on the `Nimbus-Corp` Wi-Fi or wired office network.
- Personal devices cannot print to office printers.
- Printers are named by floor and location, for example `MUM-3F-East`.

## Problem: Printer not showing on my laptop

1. Confirm you are connected to `Nimbus-Corp`, not `Nimbus-Guest`.
2. Open the **Software Portal** and search for the printer by name.
3. Select **Install**. Wait for the confirmation message.
4. Print a test page.

## Problem: Job sent but nothing prints

1. Open the print queue on your laptop and check for stuck jobs. Cancel stuck jobs and resend.
2. Walk to the printer and check for error lights, paper jams, or low toner messages.
3. If the printer uses **secure release**, tap your ID badge on the reader to release the job.
4. Try another printer on the same floor.

## Problem: Paper jam

1. Open the printer panels shown on the display.
2. Remove the jammed paper gently in the direction of the paper path.
3. Check for torn pieces left inside.
4. Close all panels and wait for the printer to reset.

## Problem: Toner or paper supply

- Paper: refill from the supply cabinet on each floor.
- Toner: raise a **P4** ticket. Do not open toner compartments yourself on multifunction devices.

## Problem: Scan to email not working

1. Confirm the email address on the printer panel is your company address.
2. Files larger than 25 MB cannot be scanned to email. Use scan to cloud drive instead.

## Escalate when

- The error code persists after a restart
- More than 3 users on the same floor cannot print (raise **P2**)
- The printer makes grinding noises or smells of burning (stop using it and raise **P2**)

Warehouse label printers (`Nimbus-IoT` network) are managed by IT. Do not reconfigure them yourself.

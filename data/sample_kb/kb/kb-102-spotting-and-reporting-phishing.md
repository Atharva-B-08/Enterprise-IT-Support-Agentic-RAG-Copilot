# KB-102: Spotting and Reporting Phishing

## Warning signs

- Urgent language: "act now", "account will be closed", "pay immediately"
- Requests for passwords, one-time codes, or gift cards
- Sender address that looks almost right (for example, `nimbus-logistcs` with a missing letter)
- Unexpected attachments, especially ZIP, HTML, or password-protected files
- Links that do not match the text shown

## If you receive a suspicious email

1. Do not click links or open attachments.
2. Click the **Report Phish** button in your mail client. If you do not see it, forward the email to `security@nimbus-logistics.example`.
3. Delete it after reporting.

## If you already clicked

1. Disconnect from Wi-Fi. Do not shut the laptop down.
2. If you entered your password, change it immediately using the self-service reset from another device.
3. Call the Security Hotline at **+91-22-5555-0199**. Do not wait.
4. Write down the time and what you entered or downloaded.

## Other common scams

- A "CEO" asking for urgent payments or gift cards
- Fake delivery or customs notices
- Requests to "verify" your account on a lookalike login page

IT and Security will never ask you for your password or MFA code.

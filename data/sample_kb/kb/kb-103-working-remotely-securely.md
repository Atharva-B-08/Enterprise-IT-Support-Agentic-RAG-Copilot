# KB-103: Working Remotely Securely

## Setup checklist

- Use your company laptop, not a personal one
- Connect to the VPN before opening internal systems
- Secure your home Wi-Fi with WPA2 or WPA3 and a strong password
- Work where others cannot see your screen
- Lock your screen whenever you step away

## Public places

Avoid working in cafes, airports, and co-working spaces where possible. If you must, use the VPN and a privacy screen. Never use shared or public computers for work.

## Personal phones

Personal phones can access email, Slack, and calendar once the work profile (MDM) is installed. They must have a screen lock and an up-to-date OS. Rooted or jailbroken devices are not allowed.

## Personal laptops

Personal laptops must not be used to access Confidential or Restricted data.

## Working from abroad

Tell your manager and IT before you travel outside India. Access may be blocked or restricted in some countries.

## Home network tips

- Change the default router admin password
- Keep router firmware updated
- Do not share your work laptop with family members

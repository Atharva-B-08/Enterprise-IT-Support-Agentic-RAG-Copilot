# KB-105: Data Classification Quick Guide

## The four levels

| Level | Examples | Can I email it externally? | Can I store it on my laptop? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Public | Marketing material, job posts | Yes | Yes |
| Internal | Org charts, internal wiki pages | No, unless approved | Yes |
| Confidential | Contracts, pricing, source code | Only with manager approval and encryption | Yes, on encrypted company laptops only |
| Restricted | Customer personal data, credentials, financial records | No | No |

## If unsure

Treat the data as the higher level until you confirm with your manager or the Security team.

## Sharing files

- Share by link with named people only
- Do not use "anyone with the link" for Internal, Confidential, or Restricted files
- External sharing of Confidential or Restricted files needs manager approval

## Using AI tools

Do not paste Confidential or Restricted data into AI tools that are not approved by Nimbus.

## Disposal

Shred printed Confidential material. Do not leave printouts on printers or desks.

# KB-101: New Joiner First-Day IT Guide

## What you receive

- A company laptop, already encrypted and configured
- A corporate email account
- Role-based access to the tools your team uses
- A welcome email with temporary credentials

## Do these in order

1. Collect your laptop from the IT desk (Mumbai HQ, 3rd Floor). Remote joiners receive it by courier.
2. Sign the Equipment Acknowledgement Form.
3. Sign in with your temporary password. You must change it immediately.
4. Enrol in MFA with an authenticator app.
5. Install the VPN client from the Software Portal.
6. Join `#it-help` on Slack.
7. Complete the Security Awareness training within 7 days.

## If something does not work

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| Temporary password rejected | Contact the Service Desk; temporary credentials expire if unused |
| Missing access to a tool | Ask your manager to raise an Access Request |
| Laptop not delivered | Raise a P3 ticket; mention your joining date and address |

## Access timing

Standard access requests are completed within 2 business days. Sensitive systems also need approval from the system owner.

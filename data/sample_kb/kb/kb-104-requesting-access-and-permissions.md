# KB-104: Requesting Access to Systems and Shared Folders

## Standard process

1. Open the Service Portal and choose **Access Request**.
2. Select the system or folder.
3. Describe the access level you need and why.
4. Your manager approves the request.
5. For sensitive systems, the system owner also approves.
6. IT grants access, normally within 2 business days.

## Tips

- Ask for the minimum access you need. Requests for broad access take longer.
- Include the project or ticket the access supports.
- Request privileged (admin) access separately. It expires after 12 months unless renewed.

## Why access gets removed

- Accounts unused for 90 days are disabled automatically.
- Managers review team access every quarter and remove access no longer needed.

## Do not

- Ask a colleague to share their login
- Use another person's credentials, even if offered

## Guests and vendors

Guest accounts are limited to 30 days. The sponsoring employee must raise the request and is responsible for the guest's activity.

# IT Policy Addendum, October 2026

This addendum updates specific sections of the IT Handbook (v2.4). Where this addendum and the handbook differ, **this addendum applies** from the effective date given in each item.

## 1. Password expiry (effective 15 October 2026)

To reduce the risk of credential reuse, password expiry changes from every 365 days to every **180 days**. All other password requirements (14 characters minimum, no reuse of the last 10 passwords, lockout after 5 failed attempts) are unchanged. Users will receive a reminder 14 days before expiry.

## 2. Extended support hours (effective 1 November 2026)

IT Service Desk hours extend to **Monday to Saturday, 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM IST**. Until 1 November 2026, existing hours (Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 7:00 PM IST) continue to apply. After-hours coverage remains Critical issues only.

## 3. Laptop refresh cycle for Engineering (effective 1 January 2027)

The refresh cycle for **Engineering** laptops changes from 4 years to **3 years**. Other roles remain on a 4-year cycle. Engineering laptops already older than 3 years on 1 January 2027 will be replaced in phases through June 2027.

## 4. Security Hotline wait-time commitment (effective immediately)

The Security Hotline (+91-22-5555-0199) will aim to answer within 2 minutes. If there is no answer, leave a voicemail and email `security@nimbus-logistics.example` with the subject line "URGENT". The 1-hour reporting requirement for Critical incidents is unchanged.

## 5. New Pune office printer naming (effective immediately)

Pune printers now follow the format `PUN-<floor>F-<zone>` (for example, `PUN-2F-North`). Search for the new name in the Software Portal.

## Questions

Email `itsupport@nimbus-logistics.example` or post in `#it-help`.
